SwiftRel benchmark data: provenance
===================================

The seven real sets used in the SwiftRel preprint are in this folder, sha256-pinned in gen_data.py.

A. Phase 3 population samples (the same five files as SwiftLD; sha256-pinned in gen_data.py)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: the 1000 Genomes Project Phase 3 release (The 1000 Genomes Project Consortium, "A global reference for
human genetic variation", Nature 526:68-74, 2015). It is publicly available without restriction under the
IGSR/1000 Genomes data reuse policy (open access, no registration).

  VCF:   http://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/release/20130502/ALL.chr22.phase3_shapeit2_mvncall_integrated_v5b.20130502.genotypes.vcf.gz
  Panel: http://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/release/20130502/integrated_call_samples_v3.20130502.ALL.panel
  Build: GRCh37. Phased with SHAPEIT2 (the genotypes are phased '|' calls). 2,504 samples.
  Retrieved: 2026-10-11 by streaming the gz VCF from EBI and keeping records with POS <= 19,800,000
             (data/tools/fetch_1kg_chr22_head.py).
  Subsets made by data/tools/make_real_data.py. Every record in the region is kept: SNPs, indels and
  multi-allelic sites, with the original INFO. No site filtering is applied here; the benchmark command applies
  --maf inside the tool, as a user would.

B. 30x family sets (NYGC high-coverage, GRCh38; with parent-offspring trios)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: Byrska-Bishop M et al. "High-coverage whole-genome sequencing of the expanded 1000 Genomes Project cohort
including 602 trios", Cell 185:3426-3440, 2022. 3,202 samples (2,504 unrelated + 698 related), open access under the
IGSR/1000 Genomes data reuse policy.

  VCF:  http://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/1000G_2504_high_coverage/working/20220422_3202_phased_SNV_INDEL_SV/1kGP_high_coverage_Illumina.chr22.filtered.SNV_INDEL_SV_phased_panel.vcf.gz
  Pedigree: http://ftp.1000genomes.ebi.ac.uk/vol1/ftp/data_collections/1000G_2504_high_coverage/20130606_g1k_3202_samples_ped_population.txt
            (shipped as data/20130606_g1k_3202_samples_ped_population.txt for the pedigree analysis)
  Retrieved: 2026-10-11 by streaming the gz VCF and keeping records with POS <= 19,000,000
             (data/tools/fetch_30x_chr22_head.py; 163,947 records, all 3,202 samples).
  Subsets made by data/tools/make_30x_family_sets.py: all samples of three populations (trio children and parents
  included), every record in the region (SNVs, indels, SVs; no site filtering; --maf in the benchmark does that).

C. Synthetic data (generated by gen_data.py from fixed seeds when prepare.py runs)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The SwiftLD haplotype-mosaic generator (Li-Stephens style, LD decaying with distance) plus simulated trios: child
3t+2 copies one haplotype of individual 3t and one of 3t+1 on every chromosome, giving parent-offspring pairs.
Missing calls, unphased calls, a haploid-coded chromosome (whole sites skipped by relatedness2), extra FORMAT
fields, filtered records, multi-allelic and indel records cover shapes the real slices lack.
